## Unchanged builds, forks and accurate references

An unchanged source build may retain the name and artwork. Label it accurately,
for example: "Unofficial build of Kleiotide 0.29.3 from unchanged source." Do not
imply that the project supplied, signed, tested or endorsed that binary.

A modified fork or another application needs permission to use Kleiotide's name
or official artwork as its own public identity; otherwise choose a distinct name
and icon. It may truthfully describe its origin, for example "based on
Kleiotide", and retain required historical copyright notices and credits.
These identity rules do not require removal of accurate references to Kleiotide
from source history, documentation, scientific citations or acknowledgements.

Keep the artwork credit and this policy in source/binary distributions that
include the artwork, including its converted variants. A separate credit next
to every screenshot, small icon or link is not required; attribution to
Elizaveta Shchepina is appreciated when practical. Do not attribute the artwork
to someone else.
