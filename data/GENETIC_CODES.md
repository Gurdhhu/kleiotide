Genetic code catalog: NCBI gc.prt, version 4.6, retrieved 18 October 2026.
Source: https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/entrez/misc/data/gc.prt
The bundled original is ncbi_gc.prt. src/core/genetic_codes.cpp retains all 27
NCBI IDs and 64 assignments in TCAG order. Stop/sense context flags come from
sncbieaa (tables 27, 28, 31); these codons retain their ncbieaa sense assignment.
Initiator flags are not used to recode internal alignment codons to methionine.
